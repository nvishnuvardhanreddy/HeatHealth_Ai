MODEL TRANSPARENCY

HeatHealthAI converts weather into a Human Thermal Stress Index. WBGT and UTCI are transparent estimates in this SIH build; official validated formulations and measured radiation/globe/psychrometric inputs should be used for operational deployment. Mortality and hospitalization are 0–100 risk indicators, not clinical probabilities. Historical health data, calibration, uncertainty analysis and retrospective validation are required before operational use.
